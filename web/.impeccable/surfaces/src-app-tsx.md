# ezworkspace web: hero, COO dashboard, worker chat

Scope: `src/App.tsx` (signed-out hero, Persuade, enterprise-calm), `src/components/dashboard.tsx` (COO dashboard, Operate), `src/components/chat.tsx` (worker chat at `#/text`, Operate, mobile-first). Code-led (Operate). Direction chosen unattended: subagent run, no question tool; founder's brief pinned "enterprise-grade, simple, calm, trustworthy, comfortable for people with zero AI background".

Audience and job: COO watches voices arrive, signs the Compact, reads the unsealed team map and payout in a 3-minute table demo on a 1440x900 laptop. Worker (Maria, AP, no AI background) texts the coach on a phone.

Memorable moment: the judge texts, the 5th stop on the route fills, and the sign moves on to "COO signs the Compact".

## Direction contract

THESIS: ezworkspace is public wayfinding for an anxious change: every screen says where the team is, what comes next, and what stays sealed. It refuses the KPI-tile analytics dashboard and the friendly-bot chat.

OWN-WORLD: airport/hospital signage. Concourse-gray ground, white panels, sign-charcoal header band, wayfinding yellow only for "next" and focus, route green for done and money to workers, amber only for review. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next as the one face. Hatched "no access" floor-plan fill for sealed areas. Pictogram icons in one stroke.

STORY: the COO sees the route to the map, sees the team join, signs four plain promises, and watches workers get paid first. The worker learns in plain words that this cannot cost them their job, then tries one task.

FIRST VIEWPORT: charcoal sign band (wordmark, team). Under it, full-width route strip: big count, voice stops on a line, yellow "Next" plate, stops for Compact and Map. Below, three panels in story order: Compact with the Sign button (4/12), map (5/12, hatched with a charcoal "Sealed" sign while closed), payout order (3/12). Registry and invite sit below the fold. Changed from a 7/5 split during build so the core story fits 1440x790 and 1280x800 (brief requirement).

FORM: wayfinding signage, 4th of 7 grounded candidates. seed:9329991dc580c833. Raises: fixed state legend (orienteering), one body size does the work (acetate manual), strict 90-degree route geometry with interchange stops (transit diagram), states stamp in place and never vanish (ticket wallet), destructive action isolated (dev console).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
